# DRV8214 — layout constraints

Record: COL-COTS-0028 · Datasheet: SLVSH04 (`Refs/Datasheets` / build rung `Datasheets/PDFs/DRV8214.pdf`)
· Footprint: `MC3_COL_MAIN:WQFN-16-1EP_3x3mm_P0.5mm_EP1.68x1.68mm_ThermalVias` (TI RTE0016C, 3×3 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, EP 1.68×1.68)

Constraints only. What the part is and why it is here is the COTS record; the per-channel
signal contract is the vault page *Main-Board-01 Motor Channel*; the electrical budgets below
are the vault's *Main-Board-01 Layout Constraints*.

## Role on this board

One per leaf axis, four fitted (sheet `MotorChannel.kicad_sch`, instances leaf1–4). H-bridge,
current sense (IPROPI), ripple counting and current regulation all inside the part; nothing
else in the power path. Yaw is not fitted.

## Placement constraints

- **VM bypass (C1, 1 µF) within 4 mm of pin 5, same side, no via between** — vault budget;
  the return is the GND plane under the part. TI: low-ESR X5R/X7R, as close as possible to
  minimise loop inductance (SLVSH04 §11.1).
- **VCC bypass (C2, 100 nF) within 4 mm of pin 2** — vault budget.
- **R_IPROPI at pin 1**, its GND end into the plane next to the part; the IPROPI trace to the
  MCU ADC is analog — keep it away from OUT1/OUT2 and the VM loop.
- **PowerPAD (pin 17) needs a real via field** to the In1 GND plane — SLVSH04 §11.1 and the
  vault's thermal #tbd (five bridges, 70 °C ambient, no airflow). The footprint carries the
  via array; do not delete it. Vias under paste: filled/plugged/tented preferred (SLVSH04
  package note 5).
- Motor pads J1 (OUT1/OUT2) on the rim side of the driver so the OUT traces are short and
  the high-current loop VM → bridge → OUT → motor → OUT → bridge → GND is compact (vault
  Layout Constraints: "motor current loop physically compact").
- All four channels are identical; place them as one pattern rotated per cluster.

## Routing constraints

- **VM, OUT1, OUT2, GND carry the motor current** — size to the vault current table
  (0.83 A continuous, 1.65 A stall at 3 V, 2.75 A at 5 V per channel; VM 4.15 A all-stall
  bound by the configured regulation limit). "Thick metal routing as feasible" (SLVSH04 §11.1).
  Netclass `MOTOR` for OUTx, `VM` for VM.
- IPROPI: analog, single-ended to the MCU ADC; route over unbroken GND plane, not under or
  beside OUT1/OUT2.
- I²C (SDA/SCL) is a shared bus across the disc: daisy-chain, one pull-up set; bus
  capacitance is the vault's open item.
- RC_OUT / nFAULT: logic, no constraints beyond the pull-ups.

## Gotchas that affect layout

- The EP is 1.68 mm square on a 3 mm body: solder-paste coverage of the EP is what the
  thermal path depends on; keep the fab-standard paste windowing of the stock footprint.
- A1/A0 are tri-level: GND, VCC or Hi-Z. Straps are on the root sheet, not in the channel
  sheet, so the four instances differ — the PCB shows them as plain GND/+3V3 connections.
- Thermal: ~0.65 W at a 1.65 A stall (R_θJA 47.8 °C/W ≈ 31 °C rise). Sustained stall on
  top of the 70 °C interior is not OK without the current limit configured — not a layout
  fix, but the plane copper under each driver is the only heatsink there is.
