# LM61460 — layout constraints (U12, V1.1 only)

Identity and rationale: vault `COL-COTS-0039`; the 24 V chain: `Main-Board-01 Power`.
Constraints only.

## Hot loop
- `C50` (100 nF 50 V) is the closest capacitor to VIN1 (pin 8) / PGND1 (pin 9); `C48` and `C49`
  (10 µF 50 V 1210) flank VIN1 and VIN2 (pin 12) / PGND2 (pin 11). All on the same layer as U12,
  returns straight into the In1 plane. Loop area < 20 mm².
- `L1` on the SW pad (pin 10) with the shortest possible trace; `C56`/`C57` at L1's VM end
  returning to the same ground as the input caps.

## Thermal
- Pads 1, 5, 9, 11 are the thermal pads: via array under them to In1 copper. Budget ~1.5 W at
  3 A out in a 70 °C interior.

## Sensitive nodes
- FB node (`R45`/`R46`/`C55`/`R47`) short, on the far side of U12 from `L1`/SW.
- `BUCK_SYNC` (from PB4) is a 1 MHz digital line — keep it off the FB node and the VCC cap.
- `C53` (VCC 1 µF) directly at pin 2 to AGND (pin 3); `C54` at BIAS (pin 1).

## Keep-outs
- Whole cluster in the input corner by `J1`/`Q1`; not adjacent to any `IPROPI` resistor, the
  encoder connectors (J3–J6), or U9 (AEDR-8300). Nothing sensitive under `L1`.

## Height
- `L1` XAL5030 is 3.1 mm tall — the tallest part on the top side; under the 4 mm cap.
